## Who we are

GlamEdge ("we", "us") provides salon management software. GlamEdge Owner is the
mobile app used by salon owners and managers to run their business. This policy
explains what the app collects and why.

## Two kinds of data, two different roles

This matters, so it comes first.

**Your salon's data.** When you register, we collect information about you and
your business. For this we are the data controller.

**Your clients' data.** When you record a customer, book an appointment, or
raise an invoice, you are entering information about other people. That data
belongs to your salon. You are the controller; we host and process it on your
instructions. You are responsible for having a lawful basis to record it and for
answering your own clients' privacy requests.

## What we collect

**Account and business information** — your mobile number (this is how you sign
in), your name, your salon's name, and optionally your salon's address, contact
number, opening hours, logo, and PayPal email address for routing payments.

**Location** — if you use the map in Profile → Location, the app asks for your
device's location once so you can drop a pin on your salon's address. It is
requested only while you are using the app; we never collect location in the
background, and we store only the resulting salon coordinates.

**Photos** — images you choose from your photo library to use as your salon
logo, to post to your Showcase Feed, or to send to a client in chat. The app
only ever reads the specific images you pick.

**Business records you create** — services and prices, staff members and their
commission rates, appointments, invoices and payment method, cash-drawer income
and expenses, client records, messages, and reviews.

**Push notification token** — a device identifier issued by Expo's push service,
so booking alerts reach your phone. Removed when you sign out.

**Technical logs** — IP address and timestamps, kept briefly for security and
debugging.

## What we do not do

- We do not read your device's contacts, calendar, camera roll at large,
  microphone, or camera.
- We do not use advertising, analytics, or attribution SDKs.
- We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and we do not
  sell or share your data with data brokers.
- We do not handle card numbers or bank details in the app. Card, LankaQR, and
  PayPal payments are completed outside GlamEdge by the relevant provider.

## Why we use it

Only to run the service: to sign you in, show your bookings, calculate takings
and commissions, deliver notifications, host the images you post, and support
you when you ask. We also use it to keep the service secure and to meet our
legal and accounting obligations.

## How long we keep it

We keep your salon's data for as long as your account is open. When you delete
your account (see below) we remove or anonymize the information that identifies
you and your salon.

We retain financial records — invoices, cash-drawer entries, and subscription
payments — for <N> years after deletion, because tax and accounting law requires
it. These are kept detached from your identifying details.

## Deleting your account

You can delete your account from inside the app: **Profile → Delete account**.
You will be asked to confirm twice, then signed out immediately on every device.

On deletion we remove your mobile number, your name, your salon's name, address,
map location, logo, contact number, and PayPal address, and we delete your staff
members' phone numbers and your pending notifications. Financial records are
retained as described above with your identity removed.

Client records are not deleted by this action. A client may have booked with
other salons on GlamEdge, and their record is shared; your salon simply loses
access to it.

If you would rather we handled deletion for you, email <SUPPORT EMAIL>.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export,
or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict processing, and to
complain to your data protection authority. Most of this you can do in the app;
for anything else, email <SUPPORT EMAIL> and we will respond within <30> days.

## Children

GlamEdge Owner is a business tool and is not directed at children. We do not
knowingly collect data from anyone under 16.

## Security

Sign-in tokens are held in the iOS Keychain. Traffic between the app and our
servers uses HTTPS. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable
technical and organizational measures to protect your data.
