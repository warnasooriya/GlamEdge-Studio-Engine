Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GlamEdge ("GlamEdge", "we", "us") collects, uses, and shares information through the GlamEdge website, the GlamEdge Owner mobile app, and related services (together, the "Service"). GlamEdge is a booking and business-management platform for salons and beauty studios in Sri Lanka. By using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here.

1. Who this applies to

The Service has two kinds of users, and this policy covers both:

  • Salon owners — businesses that register a salon profile and use GlamEdge (web dashboard and the GlamEdge Owner mobile app) to manage bookings, staff, services, billing, and their public profile.
  • Customers — people who browse salon profiles and book appointments, either as a guest or with a customer account.

3. How we use this information

  • To create and secure accounts, and verify phone numbers via one-time passcodes (OTP).
  • To operate the core booking flow: showing salon availability, creating and managing appointments, and sending booking confirmations, reminders, and reschedule notices.
  • To let salon owners run their business: services, staff, walk-in sales, invoicing, and daily ledger records.
  • To display a salon's public profile, including its Showcase Feed and location on a map, to prospective customers.
  • To send transactional notifications by WhatsApp, SMS, and push notification (e.g. "your booking is confirmed", "a customer just booked with you").
  • To process payments a customer sends to a salon owner via PayPal.
  • To respond to support requests and investigate abuse or policy violations.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use booking or customer data for third-party advertising.

4. Third-party services we use

Certain features are provided by third-party services, each of which processes a limited slice of data on our behalf, under their own privacy terms:

  • Amazon Web Services (S3) — stores uploaded photos/videos, salon logos, and generated invoices/receipts.
  • PayPal — processes customer payments made to a salon's PayPal account.
  • Meta / WhatsApp Cloud API — delivers booking and billing messages sent via WhatsApp, when a salon has WhatsApp messaging enabled.
  • Google Maps & Places API — powers salon address search and the map used to pin a salon's location.
  • Expo push notification service — delivers push notifications to the GlamEdge Owner mobile app.
  • SMS/OTP providers (Notify.lk, Text.lk) — deliver one-time passcodes by SMS during sign-in.

5. Data retention

We retain account and booking data for as long as an account is active, plus a reasonable period afterward for accounting, dispute-resolution, and legal-compliance purposes. A salon owner can request deletion of their salon account and associated data, or a customer can request deletion of their customer account, by contacting us using the details below.

6. Your choices

  • You can review and update your profile information at any time from the app or web dashboard.
  • You can disable push notifications from your device's system settings.
  • Location access on the mobile app is optional and only used when you explicitly tap "Use current location."
  • You can request a copy of, or the deletion of, your personal data by contacting us.

7. Children's privacy

The Service is intended for business owners and adult customers. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

We use industry-standard measures — encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), access-controlled infrastructure, and hashed/OTP-based authentication rather than stored passwords — to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Effective date" above, and where appropriate we'll provide additional notice in the app or by email.
